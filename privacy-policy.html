Privacy Policy

nonna · Effective September 25, 2026

The short version: nonna collects nothing. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party code, and it makes no network requests of its own. Everything the app knows stays on your device unless you personally choose to share it.

1. No Data Collection

nonna does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or usage data — from anyone, of any age. The app contains no networking code of its own and no analytics of any kind; it makes no requests to us or to anybody else. Its only network activity is Apple's own App Store system, used solely to sell, renew and restore the subscription and purchases described in section 5. Apple handles those transactions; we receive no personal information from them. A world you have unlocked plays in airplane mode.

2. What Stays on Your Device

To make the game work, nonna stores a small amount of information locally on your device: game progress, settings (such as sound on or off), and a log of play events (rounds played, moves made). This information never leaves your device on its own. It is not visible to us or to anyone else.

3. Sharing Play Data Is Always Your Choice

The grown-ups area, behind a parental gate, includes an "Export log" option that produces a single file describing play activity on the device. Sharing this file happens only when you choose to, through the standard iOS share sheet, to a destination you pick (such as email or AirDrop). We never receive a copy unless you deliberately send us one.

4. Children's Privacy

nonna is designed for young children, and the strongest protection we can offer is simple: the app collects nothing from anyone. Because no data is collected or transmitted, no personal information about children (or adults) is ever gathered, stored by us, or shared with third parties. The app never asks a child — or an adult — for a name, an email address, a birthdate, a photo, or a location, and it contains no messaging, chat, social features or user-generated content.

5. No Third-Party Services

nonna contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics tools, no advertising networks, and no external content.

nonna offers one optional auto-renewable subscription, All Worlds, sold, renewed and restored through Apple's App Store. It opens every paid learning world (Multiplication, Subtraction, Division and Fractions), renews monthly until you cancel it in your Apple Account settings, and is covered by Family Sharing. Families who bought Multiplication World or Subtraction World as a one-time purchase in an earlier version keep every world, with no subscription needed. Every purchasing opportunity sits behind a parental gate: a child never sees a price, and the store cannot be reached without an adult answering the gate's question first. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether a subscription or purchase is active; we never see your payment details or any other personal information.

6. Deleting Your Data

All of nonna's data lives inside the app on your device. Deleting the app deletes everything. The app also includes a "Clear data" option that erases the play log and session records on demand.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the effective date above will change accordingly.

8. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to its conflict of law provisions.
