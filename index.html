nonna

Support · Updated November 15, 2026

nonna is a calm math game for young children, where every number is a cat. Addition is free, all of it. One subscription, All Worlds, opens multiplication, subtraction, division, fractions and geometry. No ads, no accounts, and nothing leaves the device.

How to play

Tap cats that add up to the target at the top of the screen. When they match they combine, and new cats arrive from the pile. Clear the whole pile before the board fills and the round is won. A hand shows one move at the start of each new stage, so there is nothing to read and nothing to set up.

Each of the other worlds has its own way of playing: drag out a rectangle of cats in Multiplication, sweep cats away in Subtraction, deal them out fairly in Division, cut a cushion into equal pieces in Fractions, and stretch a band from peg to peg in Geometry. Every world gives one free round.

If dragging is hard

From version 0.5.2, a grown-up can switch on Tap to build: tap the gear, answer the grown-ups check, and turn it on. A tap then does what a drag does in Multiplication, Subtraction, Division and Fractions. Tap the place where the block should end and the cats arrive. In Subtraction, tap a cat and it leaves with every cat before it, and tap an empty place to bring cats back. In Fractions, two arrows under the cushion give more or fewer pieces, and a tap on a piece takes it. Tap to build is off unless you turn it on. Addition never needs a drag; Geometry still uses one.

Where are the settings?

Tap the gear. A grown-ups check asks you to type four numbers that are written out in words, which keeps a child who cannot read yet on the game. Inside you can turn sound and vibration on or off, set a round timer (off unless you choose a time), choose the geometry grade, change the app icon, start over, and open Worlds, which is the store.

All Worlds, the subscription

All Worlds opens every paid world, and every world still to come. The price is shown before you subscribe, and it renews monthly until you cancel. If your Apple Account has not had it before, the first week is free. One subscription covers your whole family through Family Sharing.

To subscribe: tap the gear, then Worlds.

To cancel or change it: tap the gear, then Worlds, then Manage subscription. You can also open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

To restore it on a new device or after reinstalling: tap the gear, then Worlds, then Restore purchases.

Bought a world before? Families who bought Multiplication World or Subtraction World as a one-time purchase keep every world, with no subscription. Restore purchases brings that back too.

Waiting for approval? With Ask to Buy, every world opens as soon as the purchase is approved.

If the subscription ends, the paid worlds close again. Addition stays free, and nothing your child has earned is taken away.

If the store cannot be reached, check the connection and try again. Nothing is wrong with the game, and worlds that are already open keep working without a connection.

Progress and the Yard

Progress is saved on the device the moment a round is won, and the cats your child adopts live in the Yard. nonna has no accounts and does not sync between devices. Start over, in the grown-ups area, goes back to the very beginning of the game.

Play data

nonna keeps a simple log of play (rounds, moves and progress) on the device only. To share it, with a teacher for example, open the grown-ups area and press and hold About for three seconds. The Lab opens, where Export log makes a single file that you send through the share sheet. nonna never sends it anywhere itself.

Clear data, in the same place, erases the log along with the adopted cats, coins, Yard items, paintings and geometry grade placement on that device.
